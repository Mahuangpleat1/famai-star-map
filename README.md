## 🧠 What Does famai-star-map Do?

This is your **local-first 3D knowledge graph** for studying Chinese law. Think of it as a beautiful, interactive star map where every star is a legal concept, case, or regulation—and you can see exactly how they all connect.

### Key Features You'll Love:

- **🔭 3D Concept Visualization** – Explore Chinese legal topics as a stunning 3D starfield. Nodes light up and connect as you discover relationships between different laws and principles.

- **🔑 Bring Your Own AI Key (BYOK)** – Connect your own AI service (like OpenAI or Anthropic) to get smart explanations, summaries, and study hints. Your key stays on your device—nothing is stored online.

- **📝 Note Integration** – Works seamlessly with your existing Obsidian vault. Import your notes and see them mapped directly onto the knowledge graph.

- **📊 Export Study Reports** – Generate beautiful, shareable reports of your study progress. Perfect for exams, teaching, or showing off your legal mastery.

- **🔒 Local-First Privacy** – All your data lives on your computer. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your study stays private.

## 📚 How to Use famai-star-map

### First Launch
When you open the app for the first time, you'll see a blank starfield. Don't worry—you're ready to start exploring.

### Add Your AI Key (Optional but Recommended)
1. Click the **Settings** gear icon (bottom left corner).
2. Find the **"AI Provider"** section.
3. Paste your API key from your AI service provider.
4. Click **Save**. That's it!

### Explore the Knowledge Base
The app comes with a built-in starter pack of core Chinese legal concepts. Use your mouse:
- **Left-click + drag** to rotate the 3D view
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out
- **Right-click** on any star/node to see details

### Connect Your Notes
If you use Obsidian:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Obsidian Vault**.
2. Point the app to your vault folder.
3. Your notes will appear as colored nodes, connected to relevant legal concepts.

## 🛡️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

### "I can't find the file after downloading"
Check your **Downloads** folder again. If you used a different browser (like Edge or Chrome), they may save to slightly different locations. Use the search bar in File Explorer and type "famai" to find it.

### "Windows says this app is unknown"
Click **"More info"** → **"Run anyway"**. This happens because the app is new and not yet widely recognized. It's completely safe.

### "The app won't start"
Make sure your Windows is updated to at least version 10 (from 2020 or newer). Try right-clicking the icon and selecting **"Run as administrator"**.

### "The 3D view is laggy"
Close other heavy programs (browsers, games). For best performance, use a computer with at least 8GB of RAM.

### "My AI key isn't working"
Double-check that you copied the full key with no extra spaces. Also verify your AI provider's service is active and you have credits/balance.
